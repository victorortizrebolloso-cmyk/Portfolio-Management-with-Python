# Portfolio-Management-with-Python
In this repository, you are going to be able to know how my group and I managed to be the fith place at a Portfolio Management course competition in Universidad Autónoma de Madrid . You will get to know concepts related to data cleaning, statistical and financial topics applied to asset optimization strategies.

You will discover useful Python libraries such as numpy, matplotlib, yfinance, missingno, cvxpy, sklearn and others. These are key drivers when developing asset optimization strategies, since they allow you to perform vectorized and optimized calculations, download and show your results. When it comes to finance, cvxpy and skelearn are your best friends. They are employed to optimize your portfolio based on your objectives either maximizing returns or minimizing variance, plus other strategies you will see. Also, you will take advantage from the LedoitWolf covariance matrix, which makes winsorized calculations so your matrix is not biased by outliers coming from past results, being able to rely on more reliable numbers.
